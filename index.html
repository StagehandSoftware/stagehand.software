Stagehand Software

Located in Nashville, TN, Stagehand Software specializes in developing software and graphical user interfaces for JUCE based applications and audio plugins.

The founder, Nicole Alassandro, has over 10 years experience developing professional audio products that have been utilized in major music projects, film productions, and featured in Sound On Sound Magazine and Recording Magazine.

She has built projects for several audio companies including:

  • Harrison Audio
  • Empirical Labs
  • Schwabe Digital
  • Veva Sound
  • Hack Audio

Notable Projects

HiFAL2


HiFAL is a high frequency dynamics processing tool that smooths out brightness without dulling your mix. Stagehand built the real-time analyzer graphical display, as well as provided graphics code optimizations that reduced CPU usage by 50%.
